About Market Mate

Market Mate is a simple web application created to help mama mboga (small-scale vegetable and grocery sellers in Kenya) reduce daily losses and increase profits.

Problems Solved

  • Reduces losses from spoiled goods
  • Accurate sales and profit calculation
  • Helps identify best-selling items

Features

  • Add and manage stock
  • Record sales and update stock automatically
  • View summary dashboard
  • Data saved in the browser using localStorage
